Privacy Policy

For the SujoodSync mobile application

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Last updated: 12 November 2026
Important: This policy describes the current SujoodSync features and the services currently planned for the app. Sujood Labs will update it whenever data practices or app features change. Please review it before relying on it as a final legal document.

Sujood Labs ("we", "us", or "our") provides the SujoodSync mobile application ("SujoodSync" or the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses or processes, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we process

Google account information. When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication processes your Google account identifier (UID), name, email address and authentication information needed to sign you in. We use this information to identify your account and provide access to the App.

Trial information. To administer the App's free trial, we store your Firebase user ID and the date and time your trial started in Firebase Cloud Firestore. This record helps us keep the trial period consistent when you sign out and sign in again.

Location information. You may allow the App to access your device location, or choose a city/location manually. Depending on your choice and permissions, the App may process latitude and longitude or the selected city to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location is used for these functions. When the App requests prayer-time calculations from the AlAdhan service, the relevant location or calculation parameters may be sent to that service. We do not use location to build advertising profiles.

App, device and security information. Firebase services, including Firebase App Check and Firebase Authentication, may process technical information such as app installation identifiers, app/device integrity signals, app version and diagnostic/security information to help protect the service and prevent abuse.

Settings and locally stored information. Prayer calculation preferences, selected city, alarm selections and other app settings may be stored locally on your device so the App can remember your choices. The exact settings stored may vary by version.

Notifications and alarms. The App uses Android notification and alarm capabilities to schedule prayer reminders and Adhan notifications. Notification content and schedules are used to provide the features you request.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your signed-in session.
  • Record and administer your free-trial period.
  • Calculate prayer times and Qibla direction for your selected location.
  • Save your app preferences and schedule prayer notifications.
  • Protect the App, troubleshoot issues and maintain service reliability.
  • Respond to privacy, support and account-related requests.

3. Services and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share or allow processing only as needed to operate the App:

  • Google / Firebase: Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication handle sign-in. Firebase Cloud Firestore stores the trial record. Firebase App Check helps protect requests. These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy information.
  • AlAdhan: The App may send location or prayer-calculation parameters to the AlAdhan API to retrieve prayer-time information. See AlAdhan's privacy information for its handling of data.
  • Google Play: If you purchase a subscription, Google Play processes the payment and subscription transaction. Sujood Labs does not receive or store your full payment-card details through the App. Google Play's payment and subscription terms apply.

We do not currently include third-party advertising in SujoodSync. If this changes, we will update this policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures before introducing advertising.

4. Location permissions and your choices

You can choose manual location selection instead of granting device-location permission. You can also change or revoke location permission through your Android device settings. If you deny location permission, features that require automatic location detection may be unavailable, but manual location selection may still be used.

5. Data storage, security and retention

Account and trial information is stored using Google Firebase services. App preferences may be stored on your device. Location is used to provide location-based functions and may be transmitted to AlAdhan when a calculation request is made. We use the security features provided by the services we use and take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

We retain account and trial records while they are needed to provide the App, administer trial eligibility, maintain security or meet legal obligations. Local settings remain on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, subject to Android backup behaviour. We will delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed, subject to legitimate legal or security retention requirements.

6. Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your SujoodSync account and associated data, email netrafi007@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include "SujoodSync account deletion" in the subject. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request. We will process verified requests and delete associated account and trial records that we control, except information we are legally required or permitted to retain.

Deleting the App from your device does not by itself delete your Firebase account or server-side trial record. If you have an active Google Play subscription, deleting your SujoodSync account or uninstalling the App does not automatically cancel that subscription. Manage or cancel it in Google Play.

Release checklist: Before public release, SujoodSync will provide an easily discoverable in-app account-deletion option and a public web page for submitting deletion requests, as required by Google Play for apps that allow account creation. This policy and the Play Console deletion URL must be updated to point to that working web page before release.

7. Children's privacy

SujoodSync is not designed specifically for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Where this occurs, the providers' applicable safeguards and terms govern their processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our service providers or applicable requirements change. We will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after an updated policy is made available means the updated policy applies going forward, to the extent permitted by law.
