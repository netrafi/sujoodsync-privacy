Account and data deletion request for SujoodSync

Your data matters to us. You can request permanent deletion of your SujoodSync account and associated personal data without reinstalling or accessing the mobile app.

1. How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your SujoodSync account, send an email from the Google account email address associated with your SujoodSync account.

Account deletion request

Email: netrafi007@gmail.com

Subject: SujoodSync Account Deletion

Please include the Google account email address associated with your SujoodSync account.

Request Account Deletion

2. What information will be deleted?

Once your identity and request have been verified and deletion has been completed, we delete the following account-associated information under our control:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your associated Firestore user profile.
  • Your account-linked trial start and expiry records.
  • Your Firebase UID association with trial eligibility.
  • Your stored Google Play subscription records and encrypted purchase tokens.

3. Information retained for trial-abuse prevention

To prevent repeated free-trial claims, SujoodSync retains a one-way SHA-256 pseudonymous hash derived from your Google account identifier.

This retained hash is not linked to your deleted Firebase UID, name, email address or original trial dates. It is used solely to recognise a previously used Google identity and prevent repeated free-trial claims.

This is pseudonymous information, not completely anonymous information. We retain it only while the free-trial abuse-prevention system remains necessary.

4. Google Play subscriptions

The in-app account deletion process attempts to stop auto-renewal of an active, verifiable Google Play subscription.

Account deletion does not provide a refund for previous payments or unused subscription time. You can check your subscription and billing status in Google Play.

If you submit your deletion request by email, please also check and cancel any active subscription through Google Play.

5. Processing your request

We will review your request and may contact you if additional information is required to verify ownership of the account.

Account deletion will be completed after successful verification and processing. You will receive confirmation when the request has been completed.

Do not include your Google password, payment-card information or Google Play purchase token in your email.

6. Delete from the application

If you still have access to SujoodSync, you can also initiate deletion directly from:

Settings → Your Account → Delete Account

The application will ask you to reauthenticate with Google before confirming deletion.

Please read our complete SujoodSync Privacy Policy to understand our data collection, usage, retention and deletion practices.
