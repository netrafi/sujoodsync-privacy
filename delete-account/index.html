Request deletion of your SujoodSync account and associated app data.

How to request account deletion

You can request deletion even if SujoodSync is no longer installed on your device. Email our support team from the Google account email address you used to sign in to SujoodSync.

Include the subject “SujoodSync account deletion request” and state that you want your SujoodSync account and associated data deleted. Do not send your password.

Email an account deletion request

Support email: netrafi007@gmail.com

What information will be deleted?

After we verify the request, we will delete the SujoodSync Firebase Authentication account and the associated SujoodSync Firestore user record, including the trial-start information stored for that account.

Prayer times and calculations are generated for app functionality. Any locally stored app preferences or downloaded app data can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Processing time and verification

We will use the Google account email address supplied in the request to help verify account ownership. We will contact you if we need additional information. We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm when the request has been processed.

We do not ask for or need your Google password. Never email your password or verification codes.

Subscriptions and payment records

If you have an active SujoodSync subscription purchased through Google Play, cancel it separately in Google Play: open Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → SujoodSync → Cancel subscription. Deleting your app account does not itself cancel a Google Play subscription.

Google Play may retain purchase and transaction records under its own policies. Any information that must be retained by Sujood Labs for a legitimate legal or security reason will be restricted and retained only as required.
