Privacy Policy

For the SujoodSync mobile application

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Last updated: 24 November 2026
Your privacy matters. SujoodSync does not include third-party advertising. We process information only to provide app functionality, manage subscriptions and trials, protect our services, and respond to user requests.

Sujood Labs ("we", "us", or "our") provides the SujoodSync mobile application ("SujoodSync" or the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect or process, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we process

Google account information. When you sign in using Google, Firebase Authentication processes your Google account identifier, Firebase user ID (UID), name, email address and authentication information required to provide your account and sign-in functionality.

Trial and eligibility information. To administer the seven-day free trial and prevent repeated trial claims, we process your Firebase UID, trial start date, trial end date and a pseudonymous hash derived from your Google account identifier. Trial eligibility information is stored in Cloudflare D1. A limited user profile and trial start record may also be stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Trial-abuse prevention information retained after account deletion. When you delete your SujoodSync account, we delete your account-linked trial record and Firebase UID association. However, we retain a one-way SHA-256 hash derived from your Google account identifier in a separate trial-abuse prevention record. This helps prevent the same Google identity from claiming another free trial by creating a new account. This retained hash is pseudonymous information, not completely anonymous information. It does not contain your Firebase UID, name, email address or original trial dates. We retain it while this trial-eligibility protection remains necessary.

Subscription and purchase information. When you purchase SujoodSync Premium through Google Play, our backend processes the Google Play purchase token to verify your subscription. We store an encrypted purchase token and its SHA-256 hash, together with the product ID, subscription plan, subscription status, expiry information and acknowledgement status. These records are associated with your Firebase UID to manage Premium access.

Location information. You may allow the App to access your device location or select a city manually. Depending on your permissions and choices, the App may process latitude, longitude or your selected city to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. When prayer-time calculations are requested from the AlAdhan service, relevant location or calculation parameters may be transmitted to that service. We do not use your location to build advertising profiles.

App, device and security information. Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check and our Cloudflare backend may process technical information such as app installation identifiers, app/device integrity signals, authentication information, app version and diagnostic or security information to protect the service and prevent abuse.

Settings and locally stored information. Prayer calculation preferences, selected city, alarm selections and other app settings may be stored locally on your device so the App can remember your choices.

Notifications and alarms. The App uses Android notification and alarm capabilities to schedule prayer reminders and Adhan notifications. Notification content and schedules are used to provide the features you request.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your signed-in session.
  • Administer your free-trial period and eligibility.
  • Prevent repeated free-trial claims and account abuse.
  • Verify Google Play subscriptions and manage Premium access.
  • Calculate prayer times and Qibla direction for your location.
  • Save app preferences and schedule prayer notifications.
  • Protect the App and maintain service reliability.
  • Process account deletion and privacy requests.

3. Services and information sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use the following service providers where necessary to operate SujoodSync:

  • Google and Firebase: Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication handle authentication. Firebase Cloud Firestore stores limited account-related information. Firebase App Check helps protect app requests. See Google's Privacy Policy .
  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare Workers and D1 provide our backend services, including trial eligibility management, subscription verification, encrypted purchase-token storage and trial-abuse prevention. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy .
  • AlAdhan: The App may send location or prayer-calculation parameters to the AlAdhan API to retrieve prayer-time information. See AlAdhan's Privacy Policy .
  • Google Play: Google Play processes subscription payments and transactions. We use Google Play's developer services to verify subscriptions and, where applicable, manage subscription renewal cancellation. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details through SujoodSync.

We do not currently include third-party advertising in SujoodSync. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

4. Location permissions and your choices

You can choose manual location selection instead of granting device-location permission. You can also change or revoke location permission through your Android device settings. If you deny location permission, features that require automatic location detection may be unavailable, but manual location selection may still be used.

5. Data storage, security and retention

We use Firebase services and Cloudflare infrastructure to provide authentication, trial management, subscription verification and account-related services. We use encryption for stored Google Play purchase tokens and take reasonable steps to protect information under our control.

When a verified account deletion is successfully completed, we delete the associated Firebase Authentication account, the user's Firestore profile, account-linked subscription records and account-linked trial eligibility information that we control.

Limited retention for trial-abuse prevention: We retain only the pseudonymous Google identity hash described in Section 1 after account deletion. The associated Firebase UID, email address, name and original trial dates are not retained in this separate hash-only record. This information is retained only for the purpose of preventing repeated free-trial claims.

App preferences and settings stored locally remain on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, subject to Android backup behaviour. Google Play and other service providers may independently retain transaction or operational records under their respective policies and legal obligations.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We take reasonable precautions to protect information, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your SujoodSync account and associated data using either of these methods:

  • From the App: Open Settings, go to Your Account and select Delete Account. You will be asked to reauthenticate with Google before confirming deletion.
  • From the website: Visit our SujoodSync Account Deletion page and follow the instructions to submit a deletion request by email. You do not need to reinstall the App.

After a verified deletion is successfully completed, we delete your Firebase account, associated Firestore profile, subscription records, trial dates and Firebase UID association that we control.

Limited retained information: We retain a pseudonymous SHA-256 hash derived from your Google account identifier solely to prevent repeated free-trial claims. This hash is not linked to your deleted Firebase UID, name, email address or trial dates. It is pseudonymous information and is retained only while this anti-abuse protection remains necessary.

Google Play subscriptions: The in-app account deletion process attempts to stop auto-renewal of an active, verifiable Google Play subscription. Account deletion does not provide a refund for previous payments or unused subscription time. You can check your subscription and billing status in Google Play.

Uninstalling SujoodSync does not automatically delete your Firebase account or server-side information. Please submit an account deletion request if you want your account and associated data deleted.

7. Children's privacy

SujoodSync is not specifically designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Their applicable privacy policies, terms and safeguards govern their processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our services or applicable requirements change. We will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made.
